FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is MiAudience?+
MiAudience (formerly Portraits) is Milieu's audience intelligence product. It gives you a base audience profile in each market, covering demographics, attitudes and media habits, with specialist modules by sector, all explored in MiHQ.
Where does the data come from?+
From our own panel, run directly by Milieu in all six Southeast Asian markets.
Can I survey the people I find?+
Yes. You can send follow-up surveys to the same people you've profiled. [Number] credits are included with your subscription.
How is MiAudience priced?+
MiAudience is an annual subscription by market, with specialist modules as add-ons. Get in touch for pricing.
Who is MiAudience for?+
Media and creative agencies use it to win pitches and plan media. Brands use it to size, understand and follow up with an audience.
